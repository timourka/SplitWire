4) Policy scaling microbenchmark
Command:
  go test ./internal/policy -run '^$' -bench BenchmarkDecideIndexedGroups -benchmem -count=1

Observed on this CI VM (AMD EPYC 9V74; Linux; policy-only, NOT WinDivert/WireGuard end-to-end):
- 3 groups:   ~228.5 ns/op, 32 B/op, 2 allocs/op
- 50 groups:  ~236.3 ns/op, 32 B/op, 2 allocs/op
- 500 groups: ~240.1 ns/op, 32 B/op, 2 allocs/op

Interpretation: unrelated application groups are indexed, so increasing unrelated groups did not produce linear packet-policy cost in this benchmark. Exact values are hardware/runtime dependent.
